# Presentation Outline (≈10–12 slides, leadership-review style)
1. **Problem & stakes** — manual grading: slow, inconsistent, unrecorded; rejections & disputes cost money.
2. **User & workflow** — grader/export-QA day-in-life; where the decision happens (swimlane).
3. **Why AI (and why not rules)** — rules baseline vs model (show comparison once data run).
4. **Product concept & MVP scope** — image → category + confidence + action; what is *out* of scope (official grade).
5. **PM artifacts & hard choices** — opportunity matrix, RICE, build-vs-buy, what we rejected.
6. **Prototype walkthrough** — live: high-confidence, low-confidence, defect, override.
7. **Trust design** — confidence bands, Grad-CAM, blind mode, audit log.
8. **Metrics & go/no-go gates** — G1/G2/G3.
9. **Economics & first revenue** — paid pilot, pricing, why export-QA wedge.
10. **Risks & launch blockers** — label mismatch, bias, automation bias.
11. **Recommendation** — Pilot (gated); what would make us stop.
12. **Appendix** — AI collaboration, self-evaluation findings, what we changed.
