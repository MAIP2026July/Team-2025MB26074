# Roadmap — MVP to first revenue
Phases gated by proof, not dates alone. Durations assume part-time effort (individual, working professional).

| Phase | Weeks | Goal | Deliverables | Proof gate | Money |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Discovery | 1–2 | Validate pain & willingness to pay | 5–8 interviews (graders, export QA, aggregator); sample photos from 1 partner; define classes with expert | ≥ 3 say "would pay/pilot"; classes agreed | ₹0 (cost: time) |
| 1 Prototype | 3–6 | Show it works vs rules | Model + baseline comparison; Streamlit app; swimlane v1 | G1 | ₹0 |
| 2 **Concierge paid pilot** | 7–12 | **Earn first rupee** | 1 site (export-QA/aggregator); shadow → assisted mode; per-lot PDF screening report; weekly metrics review | G2 | **Pilot fee** (e.g., ₹25k–75k *(A)*) or paid per-lot reports (₹5–15/sample *(A)*) |
| 3 Subscription | 13–24 | Repeatable revenue | Per-site subscription, 2–3 sites; label-validity study vs AGMARK; mobile capture | G3 | ₹10–25k/site/month *(A)* |
| 4 Scale | 25+ | Channel & integrations | Auction-system / ERP integration; multi-spice; enterprise/tender pitch | Retention + unit econ | Enterprise contracts |

## Fastest path to *some* money (before the model is great)
1. **Wizard-of-Oz offer:** sell a "second-opinion screening + audit report" to an exporter: collect photos, an expert grader labels them, CardamomLens produces the report — AI assisted by humans while data accumulates.
2. **Audit-trail service:** charge for digitising grading decisions even before AI accuracy is proven.
3. **Paid pilot, not free trial:** a symbolic fee filters real buyers.
4. **Avoid government-first sales** (slow procurement); use private exporters/aggregators as the beachhead.

## Risks to the revenue plan
Buyer sees it as "just a camera app" → emphasise audit trail + rejection avoidance; site access; consent/data ownership; liability if misused as an official grade.
